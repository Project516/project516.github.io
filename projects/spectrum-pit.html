Spectrum Pit

How Spectrum 3847 keeps the pit running at an event.

Dart Flutter

It covers five jobs at an event: inventory that knows whether a tool is in the lab, in the pit or borrowed, a four-stage packing workflow that moves every item through Packing, Staging, Loading and Ready with a photo of how it was packed, a log of tools loaned to other teams, layout maps with pins that link back to inventory, and a schedule of who is working the pit for each shift, with conflict detection for double-booked members.

It is built for one scene: someone holding a phone in a loud venue, working fast, sometimes offline. Everything saves to the device first and the cloud sync catches up when there is a signal again. Builds ship for iOS, Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.
